## How to sign

The CLA process is **manual for now**. A CLA bot
that checks pull requests automatically is deferred to the 1.0 launch; until
then, the maintainer records signatures by hand and the pull-request checklist
carries the reminder.

1. Read the agreement above.
2. Email **gebra.dev@gmail.com** with the subject `gebra CLA — <your GitHub
   handle>`, from the address you want on file, containing:

   ```text
   I have read and agree to the Gebra Tech, Inc. Individual Contributor
   License Agreement, version 1.0, as published in CLA.md of the
   Gebra-Tech/gebra repository.

   Full legal name:  ...
   GitHub handle:    ...
   Email:            ...
   Date (YYYY-MM-DD): ...
   ```

3. The maintainer archives the email and adds a row to
   [docs/governance/cla-signatures.md](docs/governance/cla-signatures.md).
   That row is the record consulted at review time.
4. Tick the CLA box in the pull-request template once your row is present.

Do this **before** opening your first pull request — a contribution cannot be
merged while the signature record has no row for its author (WA-08).

## Employers and corporate contributions

If your employer has rights to intellectual property you create, section 4
applies: either obtain written permission from your employer, or have your
employer execute a Corporate CLA with Gebra Tech. There is no separate CCLA
document in this repository yet — email **gebra.dev@gmail.com** and the
maintainer will handle it directly, then record the arrangement as a `CCLA` row
in the signature record.

## Versioning and amendments

This document is versioned in its title line. A new version applies to
contributions submitted after it lands; existing rows in the signature record
name the version each contributor signed, so an amendment never silently
re-scopes an earlier signature. Changing this text is a maintainer action, not a
contributor one.
